# Soluna — Project Brief

## Project Identity

**Soluna** is a mobile-first AI landscape design platform.

The long-term vision is to become the operating system for outdoor property transformation:

**Scan → Design → Permit → Materials → Delivery → Installation → Maintenance**

In Version 1, Soluna starts as a simple, fast, web-based experience where homeowners upload a photo of their yard, choose preferences, and receive realistic San Diego-style landscape concepts.

Soluna is not intended to begin as a landscaping labor company. The early business model is to capture homeowner interest, generate design-qualified leads, and pass those leads to trusted local landscape contractors.

---

## Core Vision

A homeowner is sitting in their backyard thinking:

> “I wish this yard looked better.”

Soluna gives them an immediate way to visualize what their yard could become.

The first interaction should feel simple, exciting, and practical:

1. Upload a yard photo.
2. Choose a local landscape style.
3. Choose budget level.
4. Pick desired features.
5. Generate visual concepts.
6. Request a real estimate from a local contractor.

The experience should feel like a premium web app, not a normal messy website.

## What Soluna Is Not

At least in the early versions, Soluna is not:

- A landscaping crew
- A full installation company
- A legal survey tool
- A permit-guarantee service
- A professional architectural drawing replacement
- A robot construction company

Those may become future layers, but Version 1 should stay focused.

## Early Business Strategy

Soluna should start by creating value before full automation exists.

### Version 1 Monetization Options

1. **Free AI Yard Preview**
   - Used as a lead magnet.
   - Customer uploads a photo and receives an AI-generated concept.

2. **Paid Design Upgrade**
   - Customer pays for enhanced concepts.
   - Example packages:
     - $49 simple upgrade
     - $149 multi-style design package
     - $299 contractor-ready concept package

3. **Contractor Lead Fees**
   - Contractors pay for qualified leads.
   - Possible pricing:
     - $25–$75 for basic lead
     - $100–$300 for strong lead
     - $500+ for high-budget, ready-to-book lead

4. **Referral Commission**
   - Contractor pays a percentage or flat fee if the job closes.
   - This is harder to track but valuable long-term.

### Best Early Model
Use a hybrid:

**Free preview → paid design upgrade → contractor referral**

## Key Differentiator

Soluna should not feel like generic AI image generation.

It should feel locally useful.

The promise:

> “Get realistic yard ideas based on San Diego-friendly styles, climate, plants, materials, and contractor feasibility.”

This matters because users need to feel the design could actually be built.

## Long-Term Vision

The full futuristic roadmap is called:

# Autonomous Landscape Industry Roadmap (ALIR)

The long-term future includes:

1. Customer device capture
2. AI design engine
3. High-accuracy scanning
4. Permit/legal automation
5. Material list generation
6. Local supplier inventory integration
7. Secure delivery/staging systems
8. Robotic or semi-robotic installation
9. AI project management
10. Maintenance and optimization

Version 1 is the first step into this future.
